Conference Paper · Paper ID: 508

Fake News Detection in Bangla: A Transformer Ensemble Framework for Robust Classification

Authors: Shubail Haque Turza, et al.
2025 28th International Conference on Computer and Information Technology (ICCIT) · Cox's Bazar
Abstract & Methodology
Bangla news media faces substantial challenges from unverified social claims and machine-generated misinformation. This study develops a robust transformer ensemble framework tailored for low-resource Bengali syntax. We fine-tuned three language-specific pre-trained encoders (BangorBERT, BanglaBERT, and multilingual BERT) across benchmark Bengali news corpora. A soft-voting probability aggregation scheme unifies token representations, mitigating individual model blind spots and delivering higher generalization across contested political and social headlines.

Conference Paper · Paper ID: 647

ResNeXt50-Guided Hybrid CNN–Transformer Framework for Early Stroke Abnormality Detection: A Comparative Deep Learning Study

Authors: Shubail Haque Turza, et al.
2026 International Conference on Power, Electronics, Communications, Computing, and Intelligent Infrastructure (PECCII)
Abstract & Methodology
Accurate discrimination between ischemic infarctions and hemorrhagic strokes inside emergency triage windows requires fine-grained lesion localization. This research couples deep aggregated residual transformations from ResNeXt50 with Vision Transformer self-attention modules. Grouped convolutions capture high-frequency textural boundaries, while self-attention heads model inter-hemispheric symmetry shifts. The hybrid structure demonstrates superior sensitivity on low-contrast brain scans compared to standalone CNN baselines.

Conference Paper

A Hybrid YOLO-EfficientNet Framework for Fine-Grained Vegetable Recognition in Dense Multi-Object Images

Authors: Shubail Haque Turza, et al.
2026 IEEE 2nd International Conference on Quantum Photonics, Artificial Intelligence & Networking (QPAIN)
Abstract & Methodology
Automated produce classification in unstructured retail and harvest environments contends with severe occlusion, illumination swings, and fine intra-class visual similarities. This paper implements a two-stage hybrid framework combining single-stage YOLO bounding box detectors with compound scaled EfficientNet feature backbones. By decoupling rough object localization from granular multi-class categorization, the network achieves robust mean average precision even in heavily stacked scenes.
